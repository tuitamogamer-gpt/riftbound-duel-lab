# Turn pacing, card sizes and tactical opponent

Local update, 2 October 2026.

- The hand is a compact row with the existing hover/focus preview. Runes occupy their own panel. Deployment and battlefield cards receive more space. At 1280×720 the crowded fixture measured approximately 44px hand cards, 49px rune/battlefield cards and 56–61px deployment cards. Previously the hand was approximately 83px and runes 27px high.
- A persistent A–B–C–D–Main tracker follows engine snapshots: Awaken, Beginning, Channel, Draw, Main. Beginning has a visible frame even without triggers, and Channel remains visible with an empty rune deck. Saves retain the displayed stage.
- Human turn openings use 1500ms per phase; AI openings use 450ms. Ordinary frames use 950/400ms. Showdown entry and impact use 1900/1200ms. The displayed turn determines pacing, including a bot action that begins the human turn. Pause, dialogs, hidden tabs and genuine human choices retain their existing gates.
- The priority indicator distinguishes Action focus from Reaction priority, names the player with priority, and marks damage/choices separately. A forced human pass waits 1700ms and explains that no response is available; optional reactions wait indefinitely.
- Showdown gets a field-local announcement, live combat totals and a separate impact announcement. Reduced-motion mode uses a static announcement. Broad event borders on hands, bases, rune panels and setup zones are removed. Changed units receive small labels, and only changed runes/Legends receive feedback.
- Card and battlefield resolutions show source art/name, an explanation, and actual public outcomes such as damage after prevention, Might, healing, readiness, hand count and points. Directional traces connect the source to affected units; battlefield triggers have a local ripple and result label. Red, green and violet target labels distinguish damage, buffs and other effects. Pause preserves the source and result text. Effect frames last 1800ms on human turns and 1100ms on AI turns.
- Source metadata travels with recorded engine frames and survives save recovery, including identical battlefield cards at different locations. It is scoped to presentation and does not alter rule execution. Outcome comparisons use only the current and preceding displayed frames and never expose future results or private drawn card identities.
- Rune follow-up: channel, recycle, ready and exhaust now have distinct per-rune animations and exact counts in the rune panel and effect announcement. Recycled runes remain briefly as noninteractive ghosts at their original positions, so payment is visible before they disappear. Mobile cards use compact symbols with the full explanation in the panel; reduced-motion mode retains static labels. Added three regression tests and dedicated `tests/rune-flow-preview.html` fixtures. The follow-up full suite passed all 1,248 tests in 47 files; desktop/mobile checks and the production build passed.
- The tactical bot estimates combat using the engine's public Might/combat-power helpers, including damage, prevention, Shield, Assault and stunned units. It selects sufficient groups, avoids abandoning occupied fields unnecessarily, respects movement taxes and preserves reserves. Damage assignment values packages of lethal hits while preserving Tank/Backline restrictions. It conserves nonlethal removal and unnecessary or ineffective combat buffs. This remains a heuristic opponent; no win-rate claim is made.

## Verification

22 new focused tests cover ordering, distinct pacing, save recovery, empty rune decks, Action/Reaction windows, precise highlighting, showdown targeting, combat projections, movement groups, lethal damage packages, spell conservation, source attribution, actual effect deltas and private-information boundaries. The final full-suite run passed all 1,228 tests in 44 files, including all 169 ordered precon pairings. TypeScript and production build pass; Vite retains its catalog bundle-size warning.

Browser checks on an isolated localhost origin verified the A–B–C–D sequence at approximately 1.5s intervals, a waiting human Reaction window, English/Serbian labels, showdown animation, and a crowded board with 36 units, 12 hand cards and 12 runes per player. At 390×844 and 900×650 the document matches the viewport and no card or decision button extends beyond it. Additional desktop/mobile checks verified battlefield draw feedback, damage, a Might bonus, source-to-target traces and pause-to-read behavior, with no console errors. Screenshots and logs are in `test-results/turn-flow/` (ignored by Git). The synthetic reproduction page is `tests/turn-flow-preview.html`; `mode=turn`, `mode=reaction`, `mode=showdown`, `mode=battlefield`, `mode=damage` and `mode=buff` select its scenarios.
