# Radiance rules wave 5: the wider set — 5 November 2026

This wave adds **59 Radiance faces** outside the two Showdown lists. With **8 alternate printings** that share their rules through the Radiance rule families, it makes **67 more printings** playable. Radiance now has **168 of 219** printings playable. The retail 13×13 bot matrix is unchanged.

## Shared rules

- **Conditional “enter ready”**: Lingering Animus (after a friendly unit or gear died this turn), Incorrigible Construct (after Show Off), Disposal Expert (no other friendly units), Firebrand Raider (other units enter ready while it is on the board) and The Exile (units you play to battlefields you control). Entrenched Boar offers an optional ready entry; if you take it, it cannot move that turn.
- **Firebrand Raider** also stops units from readying in the Awaken Phase while it is on the board.
- **New board costs**: “exhaust a friendly unit” (Ki Burst, Life from Stone, Invigorating Bloom), “return a friendly unit to its owner's hand” (Parallel Convergence, Ekko, Ingenious) and “return a friendly gear” (Spirit of the Forge, Skystrike Squadron). Each cost is a separate play option naming the object it uses.
- **Gear with Deflect**: Watchful Curator makes opponents pay the Deflect tax to choose your gear.
- **Spell timing**: Kai'Sa, Rebel gives your spells Reaction while she is at the showdown's battlefield. Cataclysm stops opponents from playing spells for the rest of the turn.
- **Seraphic Wyvern**: each channel instance adds one exhausted rune per Wyvern you control.
- **Restricted battlefield targets**: Rally for War and Cataclysm accept only an occupied enemy battlefield.
- **Counter restrictions**: Hush counters only spells with a Power cost of 2 or more.
- **Top-deck plays** now look at one card unless a count is given. Earlier, a play without a count looked at no cards. Clocktower Guardian's banished card stays banished if you do not play it.

## Remaining work

They include Scorched Earth, Forbidden Technique, Runeblade Anvil, Mordekaiser (Twice Slain), Ekko (Revolutionary), Ziggs, Ahri, Riven, K'Sante, Orianna (Mechanical Marvel), Chronobreak, Lady of Clockwork, The Ball, Neeko and Storm Raptor.
