# Practice guide

The control names below use the English language setting. Decks, unfinished drafts, lesson progress, match history and notes are stored in this browser.

## Find cards

Open **Cards**, or search inside **Deck builder**. Search covers names, rules text, tags, keywords, sets and card IDs. Several terms must all match; use quotes for a phrase, such as `"Wind Wall"` or `"draw 1"`. Letter case, accents and punctuation are normalized.

You can paste printing IDs such as `ogn-049-298`, alternate printing IDs, or compact collector codes. The builder groups equivalent rules printings. In Cards, **Base printings only** keeps base faces while still finding them through alternate printing IDs.

Use the domain, set and card-type filters to narrow the result. **Sort cards** offers **Name · A–Z**, **Energy · low to high**, **Energy · high to low**, and **Might · high to low**. Cards without a printed value appear after cards with one. **Reset filters** restores the starting view.

## Build and recover a deck

Open **Build a deck**, choose **Start from a deck**, and edit its list. On the Main deck, Sideboard or Runes tab, enable **Can add to this section** to show cards that can currently be added under the deck's domain, format, copy and capacity limits. Review the deck issues before **Save deck** or **Update deck**; this filter does not certify the whole list.

**Undo edit** and **Redo edit** keep up to 40 edits while the builder remains open. Use Ctrl/⌘+Z to undo and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y to redo when focus is outside inputs, selects and dialogs. Text fields keep their normal text undo. A new edit after Undo clears the Redo path. Edit history ends when you leave or reload the builder.

There is one shared unfinished-draft backup in this browser. If a recovery banner appears, choose **Restore draft** to load it, or **Discard stored draft** to remove that backup and continue with the visible deck. Restoring requires an explicit choice; review the recovered list before saving it.

If another tab changes the backup, your visible cards remain in place and automatic replacement pauses until you choose what to do with the stored draft. Use **Export text** or **Download .txt** to keep the visible list before resolving a conflict.

An unreadable backup stays untouched until you choose **Clear unreadable backup**. The text export contains your current visible list. If draft backup or deck saving fails, keep an exported copy before closing the page. Read the separate save and backup messages: a deck can be saved successfully while backup cleanup fails. An unfinished draft can be backed up before its deck issues are fixed.

## Learn with the six lessons

Open **Training lab** and follow **Exercise steps** plus the current hint. The checklist follows the lesson's phase, and successful completion replaces the active hint with the result explanation.

| Lesson                     | What to do                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deploy your first unit     | Select the unit in hand and choose its legal play into your base.                                                      |
| Move together and conquer  | Prepare a group, include both ready units, confirm movement, then pass the showdown.                                   |
| Answer a spell             | Answer the pending Hextech Ray with Wind Wall, then pass until the chain resolves.                                     |
| Reveal a Hidden threat     | Reveal your Blastcone Fae and choose the enemy at the same battlefield.                                                |
| Assign lethal damage       | Use the displayed Might, existing damage and prevention to defeat both defenders. Either legal assignment order works. |
| Hold for the winning point | End the turn with battlefield control intact and score the hold point on your next turn.                               |

The damage lesson shows why its shielded defender needs 6 damage and its wounded defender needs 2. Assigned amounts update as you make choices. A premature single-unit move or missed counter window gives a specific Undo suggestion.

Use **Undo practice move** to try another line or **Retry exercise** to reset the position. These controls affect the lesson. On a phone, **Go to practice moves** and **View practice board** move focus between the two areas. If progress cannot be stored, **Retry saving progress** tries the write again.

## Review completed matches

Open **Match history** and choose a saved match. **Perspective** shows what the selected player could see in each frame. **Jump to turn**, **Previous key moment** and **Next key moment** move through the actual recorded play.

In **Move list**, **Show moves by** filters **Both players**, **You** or **Opponent**, relative to the selected perspective. **Key moments only** narrows the list. Long lists show 40 moves per page with **Previous moves** and **Next moves**; seeking follows the active move's page.

Use **Play replay**, **Pause replay**, the frame arrows or the position slider. **Playback speed** offers 0.5×, 1× and 2×. Focus the replay caption to use Left/Right for one frame, Home/End for the beginning/end, and Space to play or pause.

Choose **Bookmark this frame**, enter an optional note, and press **Save bookmark**. Existing notes use **Edit bookmark**. Playback pauses while editing, and saving keeps the frame where editing began. Save before navigating away: changing frames closes the editor. Each retained match supports up to 10 bookmarks.

Bookmarks stay local and are separate from replay exports. Deleting or evicting a match also cleans up its notes. If notes cannot be read, **Export bookmark data** saves the untouched stored data; **Reset unreadable bookmarks** offers an explicit reset with cancellation. Resetting notes keeps the matches. A failed note write preserves previous data and displays an error.

History retains up to the 20 newest completed matches by their recorded finish dates; large records can reduce that count. An older imported replay can be valid and still fall outside retention limits. Read the import status, and export backups before removing saved matches to make room. Imports also check replay compatibility and legal reconstruction; errors do not silently replace a valid archive.

**Export replay** and **Export history backup** contain the complete original match, including both players' private cards. Consider that data before sharing a file. The perspective-limited viewer does not redact an export. Bookmark notes are not included in those canonical replay backups.

## Use the hand on a phone

Use **Browse hand** to open the larger hand panel. **Details**, or the card artwork in that panel, opens the card's information. **Select card** chooses a card with an available play and returns to the table; confirm the move in the table's decision area.

Cards show **Available play** or **No available play** for the current position. **Check availability** explains an unavailable card while keeping the hand panel open. Selection and availability follow the current legal actions; inspecting a card does not commit a move. During the opening hand, use **Replace this card** or **Keep this card** for mulligan choices.

Use **Back to table** to close the hand panel. Previous/next hand-card controls and horizontal scrolling let you reach the rest of your hand.

## Prepare offline practice

Open the app while connected, select the player and AI decks in setup, and choose **Download selected decks**. This caches images for those selected lists. Wait for the completed cached/unavailable counts; retry connection or storage errors while online. If offline setup is still loading, try again shortly.

**Install app** uses the browser's install prompt or shows instructions. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Offline practice uses the locally available app and images. Private rooms require a connection and the server storage described in [Online duels](ONLINE-DUELS.md).
