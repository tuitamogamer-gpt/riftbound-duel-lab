The [Spiritforged FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/riftbound-spiritforged-faq/) confirms that Equipment grants text to the unit, each independent instance remains an ability, and Quick-Draw uses a play trigger. Equipment attachment targets are declared before responses. The equipped unit owns inherited attack/defend, conquer/hold and move triggers; each piece contributes independently. Destroying or moving Equipment removes future benefits, while already-created abilities remain on the chain. Recurve Bow and Eye of the Herald use the unit as source and recheck its live location for “here”; a removed source cannot supply a location.

Brutalizer tracks the most recent successful attachment turn, including Weaponmaster, and saves that timestamp. Its conditional +2 does not change its printed +1 bonus. Shield applies only to an engaged defender. Spinning Axe accepts any Power domain for Equip and its Temporary instruction kills it only while unattached at its controller's Beginning Phase. All these distinctions are covered by executable tests.

Skyfall of Areion, Experimental Hexplate, Guardian Angel, Svellsongur and other unimplemented faces remain unsupported. This wave does not claim full Equipment coverage.
